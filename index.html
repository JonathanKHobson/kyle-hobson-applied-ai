Before we talk

Do we need to know which AI tool to use?

No. Start with the task and the tools your organization already permits. We can identify the requirements and constraints before deciding what to try.

Can we start with a small piece of work?

Yes. A workflow review, a prompt comparison or a focused workshop can be a useful starting point. We agree on the deliverable and how to judge it before the work begins.

What does a prototype include?

The proposal names the workflow, inputs, output, examples, review criteria and handoff. A prototype is an opportunity to evaluate a defined use case. Production deployment, security review, integrations and continuing support require their own agreed scope.

How do you approach reliability and sensitive information?

We agree on permitted tools, data access and review responsibilities. I start with representative or non-sensitive examples where possible, document limitations, and identify what needs a human check. Please leave confidential records and credentials out of the inquiry form.

Can you guarantee time savings or business results?

Those results depend on the workflow, adoption and how performance is measured. We can define a useful comparison, but I do not promise a percentage improvement before the work is examined.

How are cost and timing decided?

I propose the scope, fee, schedule and review points after we discuss your task. The complexity of the workflow, access requirements, evaluation examples and training format affect the proposal.

What would you like AI to help with?

Tell me about the task and what a useful result would look like. We can find a concrete starting point.
