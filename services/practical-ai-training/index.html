When this helps

Your team has access to AI tools but needs a more confident, repeatable way to use them in everyday work.

What you receive

  • Learning goals matched to the audience
  • An agreed workshop or guided learning session
  • Practical exercises using suitable examples
  • Review prompts and reference material for continued practice

How the work unfolds

We identify the audience, starting skills, tools and tasks. I shape the session around what participants should be able to do.

The practice moves from a first draft to comparison, revision and review. We make space for questions and the limitations of the tools.
