When this helps

Your team has an AI idea or an awkward existing process and needs to decide what to try first.

What you receive

  • A map of the agreed task, inputs and outputs
  • Requirements and constraints for an AI-assisted workflow
  • Human review points and important failure conditions
  • A recommendation for a bounded next test

How the work unfolds

We examine the current task, the people involved and the constraints. I identify assumptions that would change the proposed approach.

I make the workflow and review points tangible, then walk through the options with your team.

Shape the scope

Bring to the conversation

  • The task and who performs it
  • Examples of the current input and desired output
  • Permitted tools, constraints and the decision owner

Agree before kickoff

We agree on the workflow and depth of review. This engagement produces a decision and proposed test scope; implementation, production security review and ongoing operations are separate deliverables.

Pricing and timing

I propose the scope, fee, schedule and review points after we discuss your project. Work starts once we agree on the deliverables and terms.
