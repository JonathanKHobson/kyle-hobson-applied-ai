Prompt development and prototypes

Develop and evaluate a prompt or small AI-assisted prototype around a defined task with Kyle Hobson.

Discuss your project

When this helps

Your current prompts are inconsistent, an early workflow needs to become tangible, or your team needs a structured way to compare alternatives.

What you receive

  • Task requirements and expected output structure
  • The agreed prompt, workflow or working prototype
  • Representative test examples and comparison criteria
  • Known limitations, review steps and a walkthrough

How the work unfolds

We define what must survive a change: required content, constraints, output format and review conditions.

I develop the agreed artifact and compare its behavior against the examples. We examine failures and preserve a record of the decisions.

Shape the scope

Bring to the conversation

  • The task and current prompts or early concept
  • Representative examples and a useful output
  • Tool permissions and relevant technical constraints

Agree before kickoff

We agree on the prototype boundary, example set, delivery format and technical environment. Production integrations, access approvals, model training and continuing maintenance are not assumed.

Pricing and timing

I propose the scope, fee, schedule and review points after we discuss your project. Work starts once we agree on the deliverables and terms.
